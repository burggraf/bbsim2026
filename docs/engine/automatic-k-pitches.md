First occupied before fewer than two outs makes the uncaught-third-strike batter automatically out. Other runners may still advance. Parent selects its defensive recovery-play meaning, supported by the recovery/throw examples and9.12(f)(2)'s distinct recovered retirement, rather than treating automatic5.09(a)(3)/9.15(a)(2) retirement as that recovery play. Do not claim an express automatic-K exemption is printed in the rulebook.
