## Primary rule and boundary

Cached 2019 Official Baseball Rules/provenance: [2019 rules](2019-rules.md).6.02(a), lines3168–3172, lets play proceed without reference to the balk if the batter reaches first on Hit/Error/BB/HBP **or otherwise**, and every other runner advances at least one base.5.05(a)(2),1065–1072, permits eligible uncaught-third-strike reaches; before first occupancy/outs govern eligibility, not same-play vacating.5.05(b),1114ff, and5.06(a)–(b),1192–1203, distinguish entitlement, actual advance, touching and final position. Final endpoints and forced awards must not manufacture touch facts.

6.02(a)'s missed-advancing-base/upheld-appeal exception,3177–3180, can satisfy minimum progress without legal touch. This first actual-touch witness is therefore a supported subset, **not a universal touch requirement**. Missing-base/appeal histories need separate facts; they neither legalize the missed touch nor restore runs.5.08(a),1645–1654/1687–1696, preserves third-out/preceding-runner consequences. Retirement, terminal/walk-off, wild-throw/protection,601g/602d and independent pitch/error/steal interactions remain required follow-ons, not new exclusions or zero-probability outcomes.

## Supported qualification and error contract

Ordinary602a + PlayProceedsWithoutReference + explicit ActualMinimumProgress, with nonempty before bases, supports a canonical PA where the batter and every before runner finish safely at or beyond the supplied threshold (Home allowed), with no outs, third-out or personnel sequence and no game-ending transition. Touch assertions start from this before-state, not an earlier unresolved origin. Canonical identity/responsibility/movement/conservation/scoring validation remains authoritative; the witness does not infer runs, outcome classification, contact/fielding credits or PA completeness.

Known state/participant/PA/role/count/review/K contradictions remain prior to deferred progress. The existing safe-endpoint automatic-K IllegalStateChange remains unchanged. Final Out alone does not contradict acquisition, because eligible after-first retirement is lawful.
