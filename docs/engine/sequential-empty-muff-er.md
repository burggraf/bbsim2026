## Bounded extension

bases, observed clean zero-run prefix, same pitcher/no personnel, qualified
station-hit/force/balk/held-retirement/HR suffix and legal completion. Permit
later fully reviewed bound muffs only when their actual before bases are empty.
Earlier error and clean runners have already scored; their virtual destinations
were certified separately. A later empty-base muff introduces just its own
new unearned reach and another missed retirement, no error-assisted movement of
another runner. Occupied-base muffs and missing review/method stay unknown.

Clean runs earn only while actual before-outs plus lost retirements is below3.
Preserve earned runs already established before the virtual third out, even
when later errors occur. After that out all later runs in this bounded same-
pitcher history are unearned. Saturating the virtual lost-out clock at3 is
only an irreversible inning-ended representation, never a cap on Error units,
physical events, runs, PA or fielding credits. Reset this clock at the observed
half boundary, alongside the live error reach. Prior unknown totals remain
irreversible. Relief/personnel, other misplays/advancement, pre-first-error
scoring, missing origin/completion and all existing excluded histories remain
unknown. The first two-out/occupied-base certificate stays separate/unchanged.
