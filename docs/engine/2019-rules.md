- **Rule 7.01(a),(b),(g)(1)–(3):** nine innings unless tied, shortened for a home lead or called; a tie continues; home ahead after the visiting ninth ends the game without a bottom ninth; visiting lead after a completed ninth ends the game; in the bottom ninth or later, the home winning run ends the game immediately. An out-of-park game-ending HR is an exception permitting the batter and all runners to score. A modeled event that would overshoot the winning run without an explicit HR classification cannot safely award all its runs.
- **Rules 5.05(a)(2), 5.09(a)(3):** an uncaught third strike permits the batter to become a runner only with first unoccupied or two outs; with first occupied below two outs he is automatically retired. For an explicit K, the canonical validator now rejects a safe batter destination or an explicitly after-first final-out claim under that ineligible before-state. A same-play advance that vacates first does not retroactively establish eligibility. Caught/otherwise retired strikeouts and simultaneous runner advances remain legal. Missing K/catch/touch facts are not inferred; this is not a full pitch/count model.

- **Rule 5.08(a):** a run does not score on a play whose third out is the batter-runner before first, a force out, or a preceding runner's successful missed-base appeal. A non-force timing out may permit runs that scored first. Runner movement to home and **credited runs** are distinct. E-03 now records the final out's runner/reason and which home crossings preceded it; no reason/timing means a third-out home crossing is rejected.
- **Rules 9.02(a)(18), 9.07(h):** CS counts occurrences, including attempts saved by errors, not universally physical outs. [Eight synthetic CS tests](../../engine/tests/cs_2019.rs) protect the current retired-runner vocabulary: repeated same-actor CS under explicit `NoMisplay` rejects `IllegalCredit`; under `Unknown` rejects `UnsupportedRule` because attempt chronology is absent, including with/without supplied Error. Preserve singleton Unknown, one CS per distinct retired actor, K+CS/half-boundary accounting, later occurrences after reaching again, and repeated valid PO/RBI units. No silent dedupe or global CS≤outs rule; no full error-saved/9.07(h) exception support or historical review inference. See [boundary rationale/evidence](accounting.md).
- **Rule 5.04(a)(2):** a substitute takes the replaced hitter's batting slot; retained hitters cannot exchange slots, including pitchers who remain in the order. Shared personnel validation now checks retained identities against their before-state slots, not just final active membership/uniqueness. [Four new lineup tests](../../engine/tests/lineup_2019.rs) enumerate 576 pair swaps per reducer/direct-constructor entry point across both teams/halfs, DH/no-DH and both change orders; semantic codec forgery moves a retained hitter during an otherwise real bench replacement. Valid bench replacements retain unchanged slots and add no PA/BF/runs. Existing pinch-runner, no-DH pitcher and pitcher-for-DH cases remain legal; no new defensive-position/double-switch support or wire change.
- **Rule 5.11(a):** a DH is declared before the game and is optional even where allowed; the starting DH must bat once unless the opposing pitcher changes (5.11(a)(2)). The DH can be replaced in his fixed lineup slot; a pinch runner takes the slot/DH role; a pitcher batting/running for the DH terminates the role. Rule 5.11(b) specifies home-league practice for World Series or exhibition interleague games; the selected 2019 MLB regular-season source has game-level DH flags in F-01, which must drive the pilot configuration rather than silently assuming universal DH. Once a DH assumes a defensive position, the DH role ends (5.11(a)(12)); while `dh_slot` remains active its player cannot receive fielding credits. Position changes on defense and some multi-player double-switches are not yet represented.
- **Rule 9.02(a)(1):** official AB excludes sacrifices, bases on balls, hit-by-pitch and interference/obstruction awards. `BBE8` classifies available PA credits for partial AB derivation; unknown PAs remain unknown. **Rule 9.16:** earned runs require an errorless-inning reconstruction with benefit of doubt to the pitcher; `BBE10` certifies only the [narrow fully observed reviewed-clean-half subset](accounting.md). Unknown, incomplete and unmodeled scorer cases remain `None`, not guessed from run totals.
- **Rule 9.05(b)(1)–(2):** a runner forced out by a batted ball, including an appeal at his first forced base, prevents a base-hit credit. A supplied Hit plus a canonically validated `ThirdOutKind::Force` now returns `IllegalCredit`; scorer credits cannot contradict the explicit out cause. Other runner retirements can coexist with a hit. An uncaught third strike can create a force without a hit, so the force label alone is not inferred to mean batted-ball contact or AB classification. Generic before-first outs are not blanket no-hit proof: Rule 9.05(b)(5) permits a hit for batter interference when the scorer judges he would have been safe, while 9.06(d)'s missed-first-base appeal needs finer cause facts. [Five tests](../../engine/tests/hit_2019.rs) cover force chains/both halfs/vector orders, reducer/direct constructor/semantic codec rejection, no-hit AB and K controls, nonforce tags and supplied after-first/before-first/unspecified-cause Hit preservation. Wire remains `BBE11`; full scorer classification is not asserted.
- **Rule 9.06(a)–(g), bounded hit-value slice:** `BBE12` adds mutually exclusive Single/Double/Triple scorer modifiers on exactly one batter Hit; out-of-park HR already supplies its type. Final destination is a ceiling, not scored value: throws/errors can add bases beyond a Single. Typed retirements need additional touch/out-location facts, and extra-base classification currently requires explicit NoMisplay. Ordinary walk-off value must fit the winning scorer's advance; multi-scorer cases compare all possible origins without using vector order and preserve unsupported ambiguity. [Hit-value tests](../../engine/tests/hit_value_2019.rs) and [credited breakdown limits](accounting.md) preserve generic hits, unknowns, actual-pitcher attribution and the HR exception. Temporary representability limits do not waive the [approved completion matrix](g1-accounting-scope.md); ER and official-box coverage are not extended by this classification.
- **Rule 9.13:** a passed ball is charged to a catcher, not the pitcher. Known-role validation excludes the active DH from PO/A/E/PB and the current pitcher from PB; it does not identify other defensive positions or certify catcher assignment. An eligible fielder's PB credit remains a supplied play fact.
- **Rule 9.16(g):** responsibility follows the runner put on base, including a batter who replaces that runner through a fielder's choice. `BBE11` now supports the bounded lone-runner retired/batter-to-first/one-out/no-run case, preserving the retired runner's charge; [source evidence](scorer-evidence.md) documents this need. A subsequent [reviewed-clean accounting extension](accounting.md) permits this validated FC shape to retain earned status only within a completed fully observed reviewed-clean half; unknown review/history stays unknown. Multi-runner replacement chains and mid-PA count exceptions remain unsupported/unproved.
- **Rules 7.01–7.03:** called, suspended, tied and forfeited games have special handling. [Replay/golden exclusions](replay-exclusions.md) now define a conservative necessary-metadata candidate audit; it does not certify canonical terminal logs or every called-game circumstance. No historical season replay is asserted.

**Implemented partial E-03 slice:** `engine/src/rules.rs` determines regulation endings for a terminal third out or a single winning run; `apply` retains the ending half/inning and the event's outs while setting the terminal snapshot's `over` flag. A tie continues into an empty extra inning. `EventTransition::try_new` independently verifies the end-state, and the versioned codec revalidates it on decode. Each `Run` credit must identify exactly a runner moved home; otherwise the transition fails. `BBE4` adds `ThirdOut { runner_id, kind, home_before_out }`. The engine now distinguishes physical home crossings from credited runs: batter before first or a force voids them; a non-force tag counts only runners recorded home first; a preceding-runner appeal voids following runners. Force labeling requires a live PA and a pre-play forced-base chain; multi-out force/fourth-out appeal ordering with home crossings is rejected pending earlier-out order. Generic `Timing` on a batter's Plate→Out move remains unsupported; `BBE9` adds an explicit `BatterAfterFirstTiming` cause to record first-base touch and credit only crossings before the tag. Missing reason/timing with a third-out home crossing returns `UnsupportedRule`. Unclassified walk-off overshoot still returns `UnsupportedRule`; `BBE5` adds an explicit out-of-park HR plate appearance, requiring a batter hit and home movement for every occupied runner and batter before allowing all runs. Some legal appeals/fourth-outs and third-out walk-off timing remain unsupported: **no claim of complete rule handling**.
