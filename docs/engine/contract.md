# E-01 deterministic event contract (2019 internal pilot)

`engine/` is a pure Rust library with no database, UI or RNG dependency. Run `cargo test --manifest-path engine/Cargo.toml`; `engine/tests/contract.rs` is synthetic and exercises the transition shapes in [F-02](../data-audits/2019-fixtures.md), **not** a replay or independent historical validation.

- `GameState` has 1-based inning, top/bottom, 0–2 outs, away/home score and team state, nine batting slots with a next-batter cursor, current pitchers, predeclared DH/no-DH role, eligible bench and removed players, and first/second/third occupants carrying player ID **and responsible pitcher**. A valid `EventTransition` owns immutable before/event/after snapshots. `follows` compares the complete adjoining snapshots.
- `Event` distinguishes a generic PA, an explicitly out-of-park HR PA, a runner-only event and a substitution. Its explicit `Delta` includes every batter/runner movement (plate/base → base/home/out), outs, runs, player-typed statistical credits and personnel changes (pitcher, batting slot, pinch runner). PA advances the batting cursor once and requires one PA credit; non-PA events never advance it or receive PA credit. A strikeout plus stolen base, and a strikeout on a passed ball with the batter safe, are **one PA each**, not multiple events.
- Validation matches each moving runner to an occupied origin (or the batter at plate), preserves their responsibility, rejects duplicate occupants/origins/destinations, reconciles outs with movements and legally credited runs with third-out reason/timing and offense score, and checks resulting bases/lineups/pitcher against the delta. The third out belongs to the event delta even though the next-half state has zero outs and empty bases; top → bottom in the same inning, bottom → next top. Stranded runners are cleared at this boundary. The constructor checks terminal snapshots against the bounded E-03 regulation-ending rules; unrepresented third-out timing and walk-off overshoot still return an explicit error.
- The dependency-free binary codec uses current prefix `BBE23` (previous `BBE22`; appended exclusive unaided out-of-park-HR cause after protected-award facts, implementation/acceptance status in PROGRESS), little-endian integers, length-prefixed UTF-8 and enum tags. `from_bytes` rejects earlier prefixes, truncation, trailing bytes and invalid transitions; it **revalidates** rather than trusting persisted fields. `BBE4` added explicit third-out reason/timing after `BBE3`'s terminal-state rules; `BBE5` distinguishes out-of-park HRs for walk-off scoring; `BBE6`/`BBE7` add DH/roster and first-DH-PA state; `BBE8` adds official AB-exclusion credit tags; `BBE9` distinguishes a batter tagged after first from a batter retired before first; `BBE10` adds required `FieldingReview::{Unknown, NoMisplay}` without treating absent error credits as reviewed evidence; `BBE11` adds the bounded `FieldersChoice` PA subtype and preserved replacement-runner charge; `BBE12` appends scorer `Single`/`Double`/`Triple` modifiers at tags 17–19; `BBE13` appends `OrdinaryWalk`/`IntentionalWalk` classification modifiers at tags 20–21 without changing Event layout. `BBE14` appends `GroundedIntoDoublePlay` at tag22 and optional ordered `Event.two_out_contact` after Delta; legacy facts remain None. `BBE15` appends `WildPitch` tag23 and optional `Event.single_delivery` after the contact record, preserving old tags; earlier prefixes including14 are rejected. The `BBE16` checkpoint appends scorer Balk tag24 and optional `Event.balk` after single_delivery, preserving prior tags and rejecting earlier prefixes including15. Its cause/disposition/exact-award resolution are bounded supplied facts, not complete histories. `BBE17` appends resolution tag2 with named actual batter-first/before-runner-next touches, retains tags0–1/all prior tags, and rejects16/prior prefixes; it adds no Event field. `BBE18` appends recovery tag5 with a receiving-fielder string and Destination for the [bound after-First catcher throw](uncaught-k-after-first-throw.md), retaining all existing fields/tags and rejecting17/prior prefixes. `BBE19` appends actual-batter NoHit credit tag25, preserving prior tags/Event layout and rejecting18/prior prefixes; it is not an AB, retirement or fielding-completeness fact. See [PA H coverage](pa-hit-coverage.md). `BBE20` appends optional `OrdinaryGroundThrowFacts` (actual batter, direct thrower, first-covering receiver) after balk, retaining all prior tags and rejecting19/prior prefixes; see [bounded ground-throw contract](ordinary-ground-throw.md). `BBE21` appends optional `CaughtStrikeoutFacts` (actual batter and event-local catcher), preserving all scorer tags and rejecting20/prior prefixes; see [ordinary caught-K contract](ordinary-caught-k.md). `BBE22` appends optional `ProtectedAwardFacts` (actual batter; all advancement/scoring exclusively its protected BB/HBP force chain), retaining tags and rejecting21/prior prefixes; see [RBI cause contract](protected-award-rbi.md). `BBE23` appends optional `SoleHomeRunFacts` (actual batter; all represented advancement/scoring exclusively its out-of-park hit, unaided by an error), retaining tags and rejecting22/prior prefixes; see [sole-HR cause contract](sole-home-run-rbi.md). This is an internal schema, not yet a public compatibility promise.

- `FieldersChoice` currently requires one occupied runner retired, batter safely to first, one out and no runs. The batter's movement carries the retired runner's responsible pitcher, not necessarily the current pitcher; facing the PA/recording the out still belongs to the current pitcher. Incompatible PA result credits and forged after-state charges are rejected. Unsupported multi-runner/advancement shapes and an unclassified inherited-runner retirement with a batter reaching return `UnsupportedRule` rather than silently discarding charge lineage. A hit with a runner out remains a distinct non-FC play. See [scorer evidence and acceptance](scorer-evidence.md).

**CS occurrence boundary:** Rules 9.02(a)(18), 9.07(h) count caught-stealing occurrences, including error-saved attempts, not universally physical outs. For an otherwise eligible retired actor, multiple CS units in one event return `IllegalCredit` under explicit `NoMisplay`, or `UnsupportedRule` under `Unknown` (even without supplied Error). The latter lacks attempt chronology distinguishing accidental duplication from a summarized prior error-saved occurrence; atomic application does not establish single-attempt granularity across every PA. One CS for each distinct retired runner and later occurrences in separate events remain supported. Do not silently deduplicate credits or infer review from absent Error. Safe-by-error CS without an actual retirement remains unrepresented; this is not full 9.07(h) support or credit-completeness certification. `BBE11` is unchanged; see [accounting evidence](accounting.md).

**Known defensive-role credits:** PO/A/E/PB may not name an active DH; PB may not name the current pitcher. Other catcher/position eligibility is not inferred: a supplied fielding credit must belong to a possible active defender, and full positions remain unmodeled. Pitchers can retain PO/A/E credits with or without DH. These are validation constraints, not a wire-format change (`BBE11`).

**Hit-value slice (introduced by `BBE12`, Rule 9.06):** a Single/Double/Triple is a scorer classification modifier for exactly one current-batter Hit, not a second hit unit. Duplicated/conflicting types, missing Hit, wrong actor and a non-HR modifier on an out-of-park HR are rejected. Safe destination is only a maximum check, never a classification inference. Classified batter/preceding-runner retirements need finer touch/out-location facts; Double/Triple additionally require explicit NoMisplay at this temporary boundary. These legal histories remain pending representation, not new G1 exclusions. Ordinary walk-off value is checked against every possible winning scorer's advance: if none permits it the credit is illegal, if only some permit it order facts are missing, and if all permit it vector order is immaterial. The out-of-park HR exception is preserved. The independent credited breakdown is not complete official-box coverage; see [requirements](scoring-coverage.md) and [accounting](accounting.md).

**Walk-classification slice (`BBE13`, Rule 9.14):** `OrdinaryWalk` and `IntentionalWalk` are mutually exclusive scorer modifiers, not additional BB units. Each requires exactly one `Walk` by the current batter in a generic PA; they do not combine with another PA outcome/exclusion. A classified ordinary walk is known IBB zero; a classified intentional walk increments IBB, which remains a subset of BB. A generic `Walk` without either modifier preserves BB but makes only that batter's/current PA pitcher's segment IBB unknown, irreversibly. A no-walk line stays qualified at zero. Destination, fielding review, and absent facts never infer ordinary or intentional classification. Rules 9.14(b),(d) support scorer classification for a deliberate final wide pitch or the manager-signaled award; no pitch/count or manager-decision model is implemented. Touch/retirement uncertainty for a batter awarded first remains subject to the existing validation flow. See [accounting evidence](accounting.md).

**Two-out contact/GIDP slice (`BBE14`, accepted bounded checkpoint):** optional named ordered retirements, fair ground/non-ground contact, continuity and reviewed error placement validate force/reverse-force double plays without movement-order inference. Positive supplied GIDP establishes one AB, not another PA or BF; known qualifying context forbids RBI even with omitted GIDP. Legal nonforce ground-hit/sacrifice and K+CS controls remain distinct. Outside errors do not certify ER, and missing review/charge/end-order facts remain unsupported. The [bounded contract](two-out-contact.md) and [accounting evidence](accounting.md) distinguish this credited checkpoint from E-03/G1 completion.

**WP/PB single-delivery slice (`BBE15`, accepted bounded checkpoint):** optional supplied handling/catcher/causal-reach/pre-steal/recovery facts qualify one episode, not all PA pitches. Actual current pitcher gets one validated WP; the asserted eligible catcher gets PB. Multiple causal advances are not multiple units, while separate deliveries accumulate independently. Typed qualified/suppressed episodes require exactly one/no matching units; missing history stays unsupported for positive new qualification. Legacy labels/PB credits remain partial with `None`, not complete official totals. K/BB awards, steal exceptions and recovery suppression use the shared [bounded contract](pitch-occurrences.md); automatic-K and ambiguous after-first/retired-batter contexts remain unfinished prerequisites. No balk, ER-vocabulary or coverage-certificate extension is made.

**Ordinary balk checkpoint (`BBE16`, accepted bounded seam):** optional `BalkFacts` distinguishes ordinary602a,601g interference,602d prohibition and unknown causes, imposed/disregarded/unknown dispositions and explicit exact protected awards versus missing resolution. The accepted baseline qualified one ordinary imposed exact-before-state award occurrence: nonempty bases, named next-base/home awards, one actual-pitcher Balk, no PA/BF/cursor advance. This is not a universal balk/no-PA or credit-exclusivity rule. Definite state/participant/credit/review contradictions precede uncertainty; other causes, richer ordinary play-proceeds progress, wild throws, appeals and pitch/chance binding remain required unsupported prerequisites. No ER vocabulary/official-zero/complete-box inference. See [parent-selected contract](balk-occurrences.md).

**Automatic-K WP/PB checkpoint (accepted bounded seam, unchanged `BBE17`):** the [selected contract](automatic-k-pitches.md) uses actual statutory batter retirement/one out and separately named final causal before-runner advancement under reviewed no defensive recovery. It keeps one PA/AB/BF/K/cursor and one matching episode unit, preserves carried R and independent Error partial credits, and does not equate an empty cause vector with absence/official zero. Canonical active-K state/participant/shape/origin/charge/out/named-run/destination guards precede unknown history; a held before runner's unrepresented Second/Third reach/return is unsupported, not a foreign actor. Actual legal Home acquisition in the automatic-out-only/no-third frame requires its Home movement/run; an absent/self-held/earlier-safe endpoint is `DeltaMismatch` before pitch-scoring uncertainty. Qualified omissions and automatic-batter false safe acquisition remain illegal. No new DTO/wire/fold/ER/coverage certificate, global final-Out/Error/nonterminal cap or gate claim; live verification/acceptance is in PROGRESS.

**Ordinary balk progress checkpoint (`BBE17`, accepted bounded seam):** `BalkResolution::ActualMinimumProgress(BalkProgressFacts)` contains a named actual legal batter-First touch and qualification-threshold touches for each named before runner at computed next base/Home. Actual touch is not entitlement/final location; fields are not a complete path or certification bit. The first shape is ordinary602a/disregarded/all-safe/nonterminal PA, including eligible uncaught K and partial scorer views. It retains one actual PA/BF/cursor advance, no Balk unit and carried run responsibility. Missing facts/richer returns/retirements/appeals/terminal histories stay unsupported; wrong schema actor/threshold/duplicate and matching before-first contradiction stay illegal. Existing pitch/error/SB binding guards still apply despite complete progress/omitted Balk. Shared canonical shape/origin/out/named-run checks are reused before progress uncertainty where their inputs are unambiguous, without inferring ambiguous FC batter charges. A claimed legal First touch contradicts known automatic-K or explicit before-first retirement; lawful after-first histories stay unsupported. No new ER/complete-box proof or matrix exclusions. See [selected contract and checks](balk-progress.md); PROGRESS owns live acceptance.

**E-02 now applies supplied event effects** to derive after-states; see [reducer evidence](reducer.md). **Remaining for E-03:** appeal/fourth-out nuances, earned-run/statistic semantics and unsupported defensive-position/double-switch/pitcher-runner lineup cases. The current [independent accounting fold](accounting.md) provides credited lines and pitcher-run responsibility, but AB is known only for fully classified PA segments, earned runs are known only under the [narrow reviewed-clean-half proof](accounting.md) (or zero segment runs), and credit completeness is not certified. The credit vocabulary and event-kind conditions are a contract scaffold, **not** a finished official box-score model. Do not infer predictive accuracy or season-replay readiness from these tests.
