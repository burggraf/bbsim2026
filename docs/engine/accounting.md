# E-03 — independent accounting checkpoint (partial)

`engine/src/accounting.rs` folds **validated, contiguous** `EventTransition`s without reading `after.score` to build inning lines. It counts named `Run` credits and reconciles the result against the end-minus-start score. `None` in an inning line means the half was not in this log; `Some(0)` means it was observed with zero runs. `complete_game` requires an empty 1st/top 0–0 start and a terminal last state; it **does not** imply complete source credits or an official box.

The fold builds player batting credits (PA, classified AB where possible, hit, walk, HBP, sacrifices, interference, strikeout, run, RBI, SB/CS), pitching outs, batters faced, classified AB against, credited hits/walks/strikeouts, runs allowed, and fielding putouts/assists/errors/passed balls. Outs are charged to the current pitcher; **runs are charged to the scoring runner's carried responsible pitcher**, including after a relief appearance and pinch-runner substitution. A physically completed but legally void third-out home crossing is not a run. Fielding credits do not create phantom offensive batter rows. Stat-credit validation rejects a fielder not on the defense, an active DH credited with PO/A/E/PB, a current pitcher credited with PB, a hit not attributed to the batter, false steal advances, duplicate hit/walk/strikeout outcomes, excessive putouts and RBI credits.

[Accounting tests](../../engine/tests/accounting.rs) include a five-transition synthetic golden segment with relief and inherited runner, a pinch runner inheriting pitcher responsibility, eight HR base masks, a force-third-out voided crossing, explicit fielding credit attribution, a 52-transition nine-inning game ending before the bottom ninth, and discontinuity/forgery cases. Three [F-02 fixture-shape cases](../../engine/tests/f02_fixtures.rs) also verify their independently folded PA/K/SB/CS, line and inherited responsibility **as represented by the bounded audit**. The `unverified_prior_pitcher` sentinel in F-02 is not a historical attribution. This is not a full historical game replay or a season-wide external box comparison.

**PA-classification checkpoint (2019 Rule 9.02(a)(1)):** `BBE8` adds explicit at-bat, HBP, sacrifice bunt/fly and interference-award credits. Hits and strikeouts establish an AB; walks and the four excluded outcomes establish no AB. Batter and pitcher AB counts are `Some(n)` only while **every** PA for that player/pitcher in this segment is classifiable; an unclassified PA makes them `None`, not zero. HBP and sacrifice counts are credited separately. Contradictory classifications and an awarded first base without represented first-base touch are rejected. Test-first: missing variants/fields failed compilation; hit-plus-HBP and award-on-Plate→Out were accepted until rejected. Three focused PA tests including sacrifice-fly RBI and sacrifice bunt pass.

**Conservative ER checkpoint (2019 Rule 9.16, `BBE10`):** `Event.fielding_review` is required. `Unknown` preserves absent source evidence; `NoMisplay` is an explicit scorer/source assertion that **all** fielding chances, including muffed foul flies, were reviewed and no error, passed ball, interference or obstruction occurred. An absence of Error credits is not such a review. Contradictory Error/PassedBall/InterferenceAward credits or a passed-ball event kind are rejected with `NoMisplay`. Existing F-02 fixtures remain `Unknown`.

The fold certifies positive earned runs only for a **completed, fully observed clean half**: the log starts at the empty 1st/top 0–0 state or contains its preceding half boundary, and every event has `NoMisplay` plus one of the narrow proven shapes (out-of-park HR, lone batter K-out, lone walk-to-first, one-base wild-pitch advance, or substitution). A later zero-out/empty-base segment start is not proof of an observed boundary; it could follow an unobserved solo HR or earlier missed chances. A reviewed HR walk-off can complete a half. Any uncertain charged run makes that pitcher's segment ER `None`, and a later clean half cannot restore a known total. Runs stay charged to carried responsible pitchers, including inherited runners. A pitcher with **zero charged runs in the segment** has ER `Some(0)` even when other players' ER is unknown; this says nothing about an unobserved portion of the game. Generic contact, errors, passed balls and interference still require full scorer reconstruction; even `NoMisplay` does not yet certify generic plays.

Thirteen additional accounting regressions/properties cover reviewed vs unreviewed plays, each missing-review position, WP vs PB, error-before-HR uncertainty, inherited runs, unfinished/partial halves, observed boundaries, cumulative uncertainty, generic contact, clean walk-offs, contradictory review facts and codec review roundtrip.
