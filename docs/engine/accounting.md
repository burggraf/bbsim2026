# E-03 — independent accounting checkpoint (partial)

`engine/src/accounting.rs` folds **validated, contiguous** `EventTransition`s without reading `after.score` to build inning lines. It counts named `Run` credits and reconciles the result against the end-minus-start score. `None` in an inning line means the half was not in this log; `Some(0)` means it was observed with zero runs. `complete_game` requires an empty 1st/top 0–0 start and a terminal last state; it **does not** imply complete source credits or an official box.

The fold builds player batting credits (PA, classified AB where possible, hit, walk, HBP, sacrifices, interference, strikeout, run, RBI, SB/CS), pitching outs, batters faced, classified AB against, credited hits/walks/strikeouts, runs allowed, and fielding putouts/assists/errors/passed balls. Outs are charged to the current pitcher; **runs are charged to the scoring runner's carried responsible pitcher**, including after a relief appearance and pinch-runner substitution. A physically completed but legally void third-out home crossing is not a run. Fielding credits do not create phantom offensive batter rows. Stat-credit validation rejects a fielder not on the defense, a hit not attributed to the batter, false steal advances, duplicate hit/walk/strikeout outcomes, excessive putouts and RBI credits.

[Accounting tests](../../engine/tests/accounting.rs) include a five-transition synthetic golden segment with relief and inherited runner, a pinch runner inheriting pitcher responsibility, eight HR base masks, a force-third-out voided crossing, explicit fielding credit attribution, a 52-transition nine-inning game ending before the bottom ninth, and discontinuity/forgery cases. Three [F-02 fixture-shape cases](../../engine/tests/f02_fixtures.rs) also verify their independently folded PA/K/SB/CS, line and inherited responsibility **as represented by the bounded audit**. The `unverified_prior_pitcher` sentinel in F-02 is not a historical attribution. This is not a full historical game replay or a season-wide external box comparison.

**PA-classification checkpoint (2019 Rule 9.02(a)(1)):** `BBE8` adds explicit at-bat, HBP, sacrifice bunt/fly and interference-award credits. Hits and strikeouts establish an AB; walks and the four excluded outcomes establish no AB. Batter and pitcher AB counts are `Some(n)` only while **every** PA for that player/pitcher in this segment is classifiable; an unclassified PA makes them `None`, not zero. HBP and sacrifice counts are credited separately. Contradictory classifications and an awarded first base without represented first-base touch are rejected. Test-first: missing variants/fields failed compilation; hit-plus-HBP and award-on-Plate→Out were accepted until rejected. Three focused PA tests including sacrifice-fly RBI and sacrifice bunt pass.

**Missing for E-03/G1:** earned runs remain `None`, not zero. Rule 9.16 requires inning reconstruction without errors/passed balls (with scorer judgment) and inherited responsibility; AB stays unknown for any unclassified PA. Credited hits/walks/fielding totals may be **incomplete** because the event contract does not yet certify credit coverage; their fields are named `credited_*`, not official totals. Defensive positions, official appeal/fourth-out scoring nuances, unsupported lineup substitutions, and full source-backed golden games remain open. `BBE9` adds an explicit after-first batter-tag timing category; ambiguous Plate→Out with generic timing remains unsupported. Called, suspended, tied and forfeit game exclusions must be specified before a replay. Do not mark E-03 done or G1 GO from this checkpoint.
