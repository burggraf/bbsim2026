## Conservative supported subset

- Contiguous observed empty half origin, including an observed boundary for a
  later half. No late-origin inference from zero outs/empty bases alone.
- One unchanged actual pitcher throughout the half; no personnel changes.
- Existing reviewed-clean vocabulary before and after the muff, with no legal
  runs before the muff. Exactly one `MuffedGroundBallFacts` at two before-outs,
  under full `SoleGroundMuff` review. Its routine before-first retirement is
  the missing third out; every legal run in this bounded half occurs at/after it.
- Legal actual half completion (including existing terminal completion) is
  still required. No synthetic actual out, altered event, runner destination,
  pitcher responsibility, RNG or credited total.

For this subset, each charged positive run is unearned. Preserve the existing
clean-half positive ER path and independent irreversible pitcher Option totals:
a prior unknown positive half is not repaired by a later zero contribution.
No public DTO/codec/getter, source download or producer change is required.

Missing current facts/review, earlier/later missing review, pre-muff scoring,
a muff at zero/one outs, a second misplay, any personnel/relief change, late
origin or unfinished positive half remains outside this conservative certificate.
These are coverage limitations, not claims that those legal histories cannot
have unearned runs. In particular, do not give a new reliever ER0 based on the
predecessor's missed third out. RBI0 and fielding evidence do not independently
certify earned runs.
