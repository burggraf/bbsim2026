## Primary boundary and remaining work

Cached 2019 Official Baseball Rules: 9.13(a),(b) and Comment (printed128–129; cached text5095–5140); 9.12(f)(1),(2) (127;5072–5089); 9.07(a) (116;4654–4665); 9.15(a)(2),(3) (130;5169–5173). Primary artifact/provenance is retained in the existing [2019 rules](2019-rules.md) evidence. Scorer assessments may be supplied, but causal/temporal/actor facts must reconcile with the canonical transition.

- WP/PB needs a legally delivered pitch, appropriate ordinary-effort assessment and qualifying advancement. One delivery can advance several runners but contributes one unit.
- A fourth-ball award alone is not WP/PB. Batter beyond first, forced runner beyond his award, or an unforced runner's handling-caused advance can qualify.
- A pre-delivery intended one-base steal alone suppresses the misplay charge. Handling-caused extra advancement or another runner's advance can allow SB **and** WP/PB.
- A recovery putout before any advancement suppresses WP/PB; other advancement is scorer FC advancement, **not** the existing batter-replacement `EventKind::FieldersChoice`.
- Recovered third-strike batter retirement under9.12(f)(2) is a separate suppression rule, not just9.13's general timing test. No retirement/cause/order may be inferred from movement vector order.
- Automatic uncaught K (first occupied/<2 outs) is not interchangeable with recovery putout. Positive qualification remains unsupported where authoritative interpretation/causality is unresolved. Likewise final `Plate→Out` or old after-first metadata cannot certify the handling/first-reach/recovery sequence. Ambiguous after-first recovery/tag cases remain unsupported. These are **unfinished approved-matrix prerequisites**, not exclusions, illegal outcomes or probability zeroes. A merely present independent advancing runner cannot bypass the special suppression rule.
- Balk disposition is a **separate next seam**, not represented/credited here.6.02(a) imposed/nullified award, beyond-award risk, wild throws and appeals remain required later work; no blanket out/extra-advance prohibition or WP inference from a wild throw.

## Selected bounded interface

Parent-selected names/tag allocation in this checkpoint:

- `CreditKind::WildPitch`, appended tag23; `PitcherLine.credited_wild_pitches: u32`, supplied validated units only. No Balk tag/counter in this slice.
- `Event.single_delivery: Option<SingleDeliveryFacts>`, appended after `two_out_contact`; `None` preserves old partial inputs. Codec advances to `BBE15`, preserves existing tags and rejects prior prefixes. Record parsing still invokes canonical constructor validation.
- `SingleDeliveryFacts` contains delivery context, handling assessment, actual catcher ID, named handling-caused advances, optional exhaustive reviewed pre-delivery steal starts, and recovery review.
- `DeliveryContext::{RunnerOnly,UncaughtThirdStrike,DeliveredFourthBall}`. This asserts one **legal** delivery, not pitch count/history. Third/fourth contexts must match supplied K/Walk and PA facts; delivered intentional walks are not confused with no-pitch awards.
- `HandlingAssessment::{PitcherWild,CatcherPassed}` is authoritative ordinary-effort assessment, **not** automatic final charge qualification.
- `HandlingAdvance { runner_id, reached: Destination }`; names a handling-caused safe-base/home reach, reconciled with movement. First implementation supports final safe/home destinations. Intermediate reach followed by retirement/other advancement stays unsupported where chronology is absent, not impossible.
- `PreDeliverySteal { runner_id, target: Destination }`; names an already-on-base runner's intended next base before this delivery. `Some([])` is reviewed absence; `None` is missing review, not absence. SB units/cause/extra advancement must be consistent; no universal SB/misplay exclusivity.
- `RecoveryReview::{Unknown,NoRecoveryPutout,BeforeAnyAdvance { runner_id },AfterAdvance { runner_id, advancer_id },RecoveredThirdStrikeBatterBeforeFirst}`. Names must reconcile with actual retirements/advances and applicable third-out home timing. After-first/automatic-K uncertainty is not erased by this enum. A missing retirement cause outside the bounded record cannot be guessed.

The record explicitly scopes **one delivery episode and all supplied WP/PB units in that event**; runner/steal/recovery effects must be attributable to that episode. It does not declare all PA pitches or game scoring facts complete. Earlier distinct episodes normally get their own non-PA transitions; aggregate histories without bindings stay unsupported for positive new qualification rather than acquiring a one-unit-per-PA cap.

Qualification is derived, not a caller-provided certificate:

- **Qualified:** exactly one matching WP(actual current pitcher) or PB(asserted actual catcher) unit. Required unit omission/opposite/duplicate is contradictory.
- **Suppressed:** no WP/PB units for this episode; physical/PA effects and appropriate other supplied credits remain.
- **Unresolved:** positive qualification is `UnsupportedRule`; no invented zero/completeness. Missing optional record preserves legacy labels/PB credited views, but new positive WP needs sufficient facts.

Known wrong actors, explicit one-episode duplicate/opposite units, incompatible context/classification and PB/`NoMisplay` contradictions are checked before unrelated responsibility uncertainty. Supplied SB cannot also describe a protected fourth-ball award, or a sole handling-caused next-base reach with reviewed absence of that runner's pre-delivery start. Pre-started SB plus handling-caused extra/other advancement remains allowed where the intended base is not itself a protected award; omitted SB remains partial, not inferred. A forced fourth-ball runner's extra reach and intended protected base do not establish an independent completed steal interval: supplied SB requires further arrival/holding chronology and remains unsupported without it, while removing only SB preserves the extra-reach WP/PB control. Longer/intermediate reaches without the necessary steal/handling history are unsupported, not declared impossible. A before-any-advance retirement cannot also assert that retired actor's safe handling reach; recovered-K-before-first likewise cannot assert a safe first reach by that batter. Other runners' later reaches remain valid suppression controls. An asserted completed steal by the same before-any-advance retired actor is also contradictory, even without a duplicated handling-reach assertion. Otherwise final retirement alone cannot disprove a lawful earlier steal/held-base interval; the typed record defers that missing chronology instead of classifying all SB-plus-retirement histories as impossible. This deferral does not override definite automatic-K batter retirement or an explicit batter-before-first third out: that batter cannot supply a completed steal even when recovery review is missing. Named causality, awards, safe destinations and retirement/timing reconcile through the same canonical rules path for reducer/constructor/decoder. Definitively contradictory facts are illegal; lawful unrepresented histories are unsupported.

Catcher identity is an event-scoped supplied role assertion, checked against defensive eligibility, known DH and pitcher restrictions; it is not fabricated from lineup order or a new full position system. WP belongs to the actual current pitcher, PB to the asserted catcher; scored R retains carried pitcher responsibility. Fold new pitching credits **before** generic batter-entry creation, avoiding spurious batting lines for pitchers. Existing clean-ER vocabulary is not enlarged.
