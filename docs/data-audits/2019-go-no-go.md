# F-04 draft — 2019 candidate data decision (G0 **pending**)

**As of 2026-10-10 UTC.** This is a decision brief, **not** a maintainer signoff, legal opinion, release authorization, predictive-accuracy claim, or approval to begin E-01. The initial target chosen by the maintainer is **macOS Apple Silicon (arm64)**. Candidate pilot: **2019 AL/NL regular-season data for a limited offline head-to-head reconstruction**, not historically exact play of a specific game and not yet a date-correct season replay. The public baseball.computer catalog explicitly calls itself a preview.

## Evidence and supported boundary

- [F-01 inventory](2019.md): 2,429 selected regular-season PBP game starts/results, 2,430 candidate schedule rows (one `2019-07-02 CHA–DET` marked not made up), 222,047 event rows with complete before-state and complete **non-boundary** after-state, including 5,508 real non-PA events and 30,022 no-play rows. All 130,392 occupied before/after runner slots have identities. One of 444,094 effective batter/pitcher hand slots is unresolved. All 2,429 starts have park and DH flags; lineup maps provide IDs, **not** dated eligibility. Published schedule does not have a verified one-to-one game-ID reconciliation.
- [F-02 fixtures](2019-fixtures.md): five representative events in three games cover runner-only and simultaneous effects. Five transition checks and 54 event-to-derived-team-summary cells matched. **Zero independent 2019 team box-score cells exist in this catalog**; that agreement is same-lineage, not independent historical validation.
- **Supplemental separately published box path:** Retrosheet's [season-specific 2019 box-score event archive](https://www.retrosheet.org/events/2019box.zip) (1,741,285 bytes; SHA-256 `34ec6b45fc2b88c6b7675d0c364bffc10e8d4f81e166ef2ad51dd65a1cd73444`) contains version-3 `.EBA/.EBN` records. [`boxfile.txt`](https://www.retrosheet.org/boxfile.txt) defines team line scores, individual `bline` batting/base-running credits and `dline` fielding credits. [`audits/verify_retrosheet_box.py`](../../audits/verify_retrosheet_box.py) independently calculates nine metrics for each of the six F-02 game-team rows and compares **54/54** with event totals: runs, hits, PA, steals, CS, fielding-team outs, putouts, assists, errors. F-02 `outs` means **defensive** outs, not batting outs; the first attempted comparison failed on this semantic mismatch, and the checked definition in `audits/2019-fixture-reconcile.sql` drove a regression test/correction. Both archive GETs were byte-identical; the F-02 aggregate CSV SHA-256 is `05f7e5d01207a44624b390207b2e8d718241c4e6deef87168f8501fe5f15be39`. These are independently published **box records/processing**, but share Retrosheet upstream and only cover three games, not a season-wide independent audit. They do not fill missing catalog box tables.
- [F-03 measured spike](2019-filtered-spike.md): 12 filtered source/local table counts equal, selected F-01 totals and a pinned event pass offline; 41.96 s cold-client macOS arm64 import, ~918.95 MiB HTTPFS query payload plus 12.51 MiB exact catalog preflight (a **lower bound**, not total wire bytes); 22,818,816-byte local snapshot. A copied 89.48 MiB arm64 CLI/extensions bundle loaded the source with empty HOME and read the local cache with external access disabled. This proves a CLI-level spike, not Tauri integration, a notarized app or permission to redistribute the snapshot.

### Reproduce the supplemental box check (all raw data outside Git)

```sh

shasum -a 256 /tmp/bbsim-f04-2019box.zip  # compare with hash above
# Recreate F-02 CSV using its documented read-only SQL and catalog hash, or use the retained /tmp copy:
duckdb :memory: -csv -noheader < audits/2019-fixture-reconcile.sql > /tmp/bbsim-f02-reconcile.csv
python3 audits/verify_fixture_reconcile.py /tmp/bbsim-f02-reconcile.csv
python3 audits/verify_retrosheet_box.py /tmp/bbsim-f04-2019box.zip /tmp/bbsim-f02-reconcile.csv
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

The box archive is a **bounded single season**, not a whole-catalog download. Do not commit it, extracted records or aggregate CSVs. It is mutable; recheck the archive and catalog hashes before comparing future runs.

## Attribution and unresolved rights (review, not legal conclusion)

1. Retrosheet's official [notice](https://www.retrosheet.org/notice.txt) says recipients may use the data broadly, including commercial products, **provided** this statement appears prominently: “The information used here was obtained free of charge from and is copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested parties may contact Retrosheet at "www.retrosheet.org".” It disclaims accuracy and notes corrections. The F-03 local manifest includes this text; a future app would need conspicuous in-app/data-source attribution, not just a hidden manifest. Some Retrosheet download pages use a **different postal-address variant** of the notice; confirm the required wording for the specific source and display it prominently before distribution.
2. [baseball.com's homepage](https://baseball.computer/) says nearly all data comes from Retrosheet, pre-1901 Lahman statistics have CC BY-SA 3.0, and **additional contributions to the data by the project** are CC BY-SA 4.0. The pre-1901 exception is not evidence that a 2019 extract is entirely Retrosheet-origin.
3. At source-model revision [`b449f7b` the repository `LICENSE`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/droher/baseball.computer/b449f7bec49f852c27803e096303a4c78ac661b9/LICENSE) instead contains **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** text, including a noncommercial condition. Its scope relative to site-described data additions and code is not clearly delineated in the materials reviewed. **Do not infer that the public DuckLake catalog, transformed 2019 snapshot, publisher code and our product all share one permissive license.** Seek publisher clarification and appropriate release/legal review before bundling data or distributing a client that imports it; no public push of raw data or binary release is authorized by this draft. Review DuckDB/extension redistribution terms separately before a packaged build.

## Proposed criteria and choices for maintainer review

These are **post-audit proposals**, not preregistered statistical model thresholds or already-approved G0 criteria. Fix them before implementation and verify any scope change with fresh evidence:

- **Limited engine-data minimum:** 2019 selected game/event keys unique; 100% selected games have PBP/results, lineup IDs, park/DH flags and at least one event; 100% event before-states and all non-boundary after-states present; every occupied runner slot has identity; explicit unknown/derived flags rather than fabricated zeroes. At least the five F-02 transition fixtures and all 54 independently published Retrosheet box comparisons remain matching on an unchanged catalog/archive hash. Any failed check blocks this limited pilot.
- **Unsupported now:** do not promise exact historical game reconstruction, accurate fielding range/arm, date-eligible managers/rosters, season replay, predictive fit, cross-OS support, or independent season-wide box-score parity. Those need E/A/S gates and further sources. The 2019 schedule discrepancy must be reconciled before S-01's actual-schedule replay.
- **Preliminary import envelope for review:** on the selected macOS arm64 development host, propose a provisional 2019 **audit-slice** target under 5 min, <2 GiB measured data transfer and <250 MiB snapshot, with at least 1 GiB free disk preflight. Observed 42 s/~0.93 GiB lower-bound/21.76 MiB meet these preliminary envelopes, but the future normalized rating/roster snapshot, total wire bytes, other networks and packaging are **unmeasured**; these are not guarantees or model quality thresholds. Re-measure the real importer before promising UX.
- **Storage/distribution recommendation, not a decision:** keep a storage-neutral repository boundary; use DuckDB as the **local, user-initiated import/cache** candidate on macOS arm64 because the CLI spike works. Do **not** bundle a 2019 data extract with the app or publish data until rights, attribution and provenance are clarified. SQLite fallback is only needed if real Tauri packaging/target tests fail, not from this CLI spike alone.

**Maintainer decisions needed:** (A) confirm/reject 2019 as a narrowly scoped internal pilot and the proposed coverage/exclusions; (B) choose the local storage/import/distribution policy and preliminary performance budget; (C) approve a path to resolve baseball.computer/Retrosheet/extension rights, including whether to seek publisher clarification or legal review; (D) separately record G0 go/no-go **only after** these are resolved. Until then **F-04 is incomplete and G0 is pending**; no E-01 or public season promise.
