- An **opportunity** is one named before-base runner on a real event, plus the
  current batter on a terminalPA only. Count the before-state opportunities even
  if no matching child endpoint exists. Never infer held or zero from a missing
  row. Extra/runner-only batter child rows stay separately observable, not invented
  PA opportunities or silently dropped records.
- Match child role/identity to literal before participant. Preserve NULL identities,
  endpoint/attempt labels and nullable flags; reject known conflicting identities,
  duplicate roles and invalid types rather than resolve ambiguity by count.
  Unknown endpoint/attempt labels remain raw observations with explicit findings.
- Keep final **physical child endpoint** distinct from normalized after-base mask
  and frame-end conventions. A third out clears survivors in the next half but
  does not prove they were retired. Home/run and Out/is_out contradictions are
  explicit findings; no invented force/tag/throw timing or cause certification.
- Preserve before outs/mask, literal primary result/PA, outs/runs on event,
  frame end, trajectory and all runner endpoint/attempt/error/explicit-out flags.
  Retain correlated joint destination vectors across before occupants, not only
  independent marginal advance rates. Empty/missing, false and true stay distinct.
- Separate lossless notation split versus structural unknown, compound separators,
  flags and error/throw annotations. The existing lexer is not a causal parser.
  A single with a runner at home is an observed single-event endpoint, **not** proof
  that an unaided hit alone caused the run, RBI or earned-run reconstruction.
- Report cells by source partition, before-state, primary result and role/destination
  plus coverage/findings. Also retain exact total event and raw-child partitions,
  no-play child rows and existing opportunity-unit totals. Counts only: no smoothing,
  probability thresholds, fitted speed/arm/range, official PA/BF, pitches or fatigue.
