## Fixed qualification predicates, before measurement

For each candidate retain **all** failing predicates in a stable list; also provide
one fixed first-reason partition in the following order. Unsupported observations
are reported, not rejected as invalid source histories. Known source schema/grain/
identity/join/credit contradictions still fail the unchanged complete validators.

1. `terminal_or_out`: terminalPA must be literal true, event outs0 and frame_end
   false. This slice excludes walkoff/inning-ending projections rather than invent
   winning-run clocks or extend their hit-value rules.
2. `notation_uncertain`: lexer must split losslessly, with no compound separator,
   exactly one matching single/double root (`S[0-9U]*`/`D[0-9U]*`), and no source
   flags. Modifiers are absent or exactly one trajectory modifier matching
   `[GFLP][1-9]?[LRSMDX]*[-+]?`. Each explicit advancement is exactly
   `[B123]-[123H]`, with unique origins, a named corresponding opportunity and a
   destination agreeing with its child endpoint. Annotated errors/throws/outs,
   unknown atoms/modifiers and unrepresented compound history stay unqualified.
   No annotation is stripped to qualify a row. Unspecified advances are not
   invented: a complete child endpoint is required independently below.
3. `fielding_effect`: raw fielding credit children must be empty. This selects a
   conservative observational proxy, **not** whole-play NoMisplay certification.
4. `incomplete_vector`: exactly one child for the PA batter and every named before
   occupant, no extras, exact non-NULL participant identities and known endpoints.
   Missing child/identity/endpoint is unresolved, never held/zero. Retain raw
   baserunning labels and attempt facts; do not infer an attempt policy or clock.
5. `runner_effect_or_unknown`: every child is_out false, explicit_out_flag false
   and advanced_on_error_flag false (NULL is unresolved); run_scored_flag equals
   whether endpoint is Home. Retain is_advance_attempt/attempted target as supplied,
   including nullable/unknown labels, without using them as causal certification.
6. `hit_destination`: batter ends exactly First for Single or Second for Double.
   Extra batter advances may be legal in a richer history but are outside this
   fixed direct-hit projection. They are not relabeled as larger hit values.
7. `unsupported_geometry`: each before runner ends at its origin or a later safe
   base/Home, never Out/backward. Non-Home endpoints are distinct and ordered by
   origin; no trailing Home endpoint before a leading survivor. All Home endpoints
   must form a suffix of the ordered runner vector. Normalized after-base mask
   must exactly equal the surviving child endpoints, event runs equal named Home
   children, and after outs equal before outs. A held runner is allowed explicitly;
   station advancement is not required. Do not repair collisions or recode runs.

Passing all predicates yields a **complete safe ordered endpoint proxy**, not
proof of unaided-hit cause, timing, RBI/ER, NoMisplay, runner/arm ability or policy.
Keep vectors correlated. Report qualified/unresolved counts, all-reason overlaps,
first-reason conservation, before-mask/outs/result/trajectory/split cells, raw
nullable attempt/cause fields and joint destinations. Report denominator limits
rather than selecting a missing-data policy. No rates, smoothing or learned model.
Even zero passing proxies is an honest possible outcome, not grounds to retune.
