## Fixed versioned count format and strict native parsing

```text

training_events\t<canonical decimal u64>
value\tbase_mask\touts_before\tcounts
<value>\t<mask>\t<outs>\t<count0>,<count1>,...
```

The displayed `\t` denotes literal TAB in the artifact. Forty-eight data rows are
in value1/2, mask0..7, outs0..2 order, one per context. Counts correspond exactly to
the unchanged kernel's lexicographically ordered full safe joint-vector support.
Every structural category is present, including zeros; unavailable source fields
are not filled with zero. Zero here means no **qualified training events** in a
complete already-validated table, not zero overall runner opportunities or cause.

Integers are canonical unsigned decimal (`0` or no leading zeros), without signs,
whitespace, bool coercion, exponent syntax or empty tokens. Reject malformed UTF-8/
non-ASCII, CRLF, missing final LF, extra/blank lines, header/version/label drift,
bad digest syntax, context/order/shape/count errors, duplicate/missing contexts,
u64 parse/aggregation overflow and declared-total mismatch. No normalization,
clipping, category deletion, borrowed counts or implicit model choice.

support/table validation. The digest and proxy label are metadata, **not native
proof of source authenticity**. A syntactically valid arbitrary/synthetic digest
